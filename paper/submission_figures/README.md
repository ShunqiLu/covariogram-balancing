The main-text figures are vector PDFs generated by `../makefigs.py`; their text
is typeset by pdflatex through the matplotlib pgf backend, so lettering matches
the manuscript body exactly, and every plotted number comes from
`ehrhart_fswa.majorization` through `../figdata.py`. The EPS files are delivery
exports of those PDFs. All lettering is embedded and captions remain in the
LaTeX manuscripts as required by the journal. Edit the generator function, the
TikZ source, or the Draw.io source and rebuild the export rather than editing an
EPS file directly.
